Metrics and analytics

ReadyYour SQL level25 min
ChatGPT's brief

The role covers the full funnel, analytics, SQL and work with engineers. Know the metrics below, prepare the "why don't trial users pay" answer, and write down honestly which SQL you can write yourself and where you'd need help.

MetricWhat it means
MRRMonthly recurring revenue from subscriptions
CACThe cost of getting one paying customer
Trial-to-paidThe share of trial users who start paying
Activation rateThe share of users who reach the first useful action
ChurnThe share of paying customers who stop in a period
RetentionThe share of users who keep using the product
Payback periodThe time to earn back the cost of getting a customer
LTVThe expected value of a customer over the whole relationship

The trap: in betting, CLV means closing-line value, your price against the closing price. In marketing, LTV means lifetime value. Don't mix them up.

"How would you find out why trial users don't pay?"

  1. Check the events and analytics are correct.
  2. Split users by source and by cohort.
  3. Follow the steps: sign-up, activation (alert opened in 48 hours), key features, payment.
  4. Find the step with the biggest drop.
  5. Check the reason in the data, in cancel reasons and in five user calls, then test one fix.
  6. Measure after the change.

For BetUnfair, look first at the first two days, because most cancels happen there (RevenueCat), and at the empty first screen.

SQL, honestly. Your call; use only what's true. Suggested if it fits: "I write funnel queries myself: joins, group-bys, date windows, cohort counts. For data pipelines or complex modelling I'd work with an engineer." The sign-up-to-paid query is on the prep site, page "Answers with sources", answer 3.

Three growth hypotheses

Ready20 min
ChatGPT's brief

Show you've started thinking about the product, without a diagnosis after one evening. One sentence each on how you'd test it.

  • A. Faster first value. New users may not know where to start. Test: presets for their own sports on the first screen against no presets, a four-week before-and-after read. Metric: alert opened within 48 hours, then the share who pay.
  • B. Partners as a system. Value-betting experts, analysts and niche communities fit the audience. Test: a partner page with written terms and one code each, with 3–5 small partners for 6–8 weeks. Metric: new payers, CAC per partner after the revenue share, payers and retention by partner cohort.
  • C. Expert content as organic acquisition. BetUnfair already publishes guides. Test: 10 pages for high-intent searches such as "dropping odds alerts" or "EV calculator", each linking to the free dashboard. Metric: qualified search visits, then sign-ups, activation and paid.
